ICE (Control) riders currently earn KES 996/day on average versus KES 837/day for Electric, a KES 159/day gap. This is not a cost problem: Electric's energy cost/km is 40% lower and maintenance/km is 81% lower, producing a 73.8% profit margin vs 59.9% for ICE. The gap traces to revenue and utilisation: Electric riders cover 75 km/day vs ICE's 85 km/day, and earn KES 15.16/km vs ICE's KES 19.50/km. Yet profit per km is nearly identical (KES 11.18 vs 11.68), so Electric's cost efficiency has already closed almost the entire per-km gap. The remaining disadvantage is a utilisation and fare-capture problem, not a structural cost one.

Sample: 9 electric and 100+ ICE motorcycles, so treat findings as directional. Source: Nairobi Motorcycle Transit Comparison Dataset, Mendeley Data (2023). Built by Violet Mutuku.
